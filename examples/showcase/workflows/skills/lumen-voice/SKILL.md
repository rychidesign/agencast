- We talk to the reader directly and informally. Friendly and matter-of-fact, short sentences, no emoji.
- Lumen is a fictional small coffee roastery and café. We talk about coffee and its flavors.
- No advertising clichés ("don't miss out", "a unique opportunity") and no shouting.
- We write the brand as Lumen; for the place we use "Lumen café".
